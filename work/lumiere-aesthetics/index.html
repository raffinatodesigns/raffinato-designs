Our Philosophy

Where Medicine Meets Indulgence

Lumiere Aesthetics was founded in Chicago's Gold Coast in 2016 with a clear conviction: lasting beauty begins with clinical integrity. We are not a day spa. We are a licensed medical aesthetics studio staffed by board-certified physicians and registered nurses who understand skin at a biological level.

That medical foundation allows us to offer treatments that genuinely transform, not just temporarily soothe. Yet we have never confused clinical with cold. From the moment you arrive, every detail of your experience is designed to be as restorative as the treatments themselves.
