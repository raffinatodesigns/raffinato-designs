Our Story

Started small.
Stayed honest.

Marisol Solano opened the original Solano Bakery with a single deck oven and a recipe her grandmother brought from Oaxaca. What started as a weekend farmers market stand became a full storefront in 2014, and a neighborhood institution not long after.

Every loaf is naturally leavened and cold-proofed overnight. Every pastry is made from scratch each morning. We don't cut corners, because we believe you can taste the difference.
