Our Story

Fire is our
philosophy.

Ember & Oak was born from a belief that the most profound flavors come from the oldest technique. Our kitchen is built around live fire: oak-wood embers, open hearths, and cast-iron patience. Every dish on our menu is shaped by what the fire reveals.

We work directly with small farms across the Midwest, choosing ingredients at their peak and letting the season guide our menu. Expect the unexpected, and expect it to be delicious.

Executive Chef Marcus Delacroix  |  Est. 2018
