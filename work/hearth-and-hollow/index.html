Our Craft

Slow made.
Built to last.

Hearth and Hollow began in a small studio on Limestone Street in 2016. We started with a single kiln and a belief that the objects in your home should be worth caring about. Everything we make or source is chosen for durability, beauty, and the feel of it in your hand.

We work with a small group of makers in Kentucky and the surrounding region. Some have been potters for thirty years. Some just started. All of them care about the work.

"Buy fewer things. Buy better things. Live with them a long time."
Nora Callahan, Founder
