Our Philosophy

Advice that works in your interest. Only yours.

Crest Financial was founded on a simple belief: financial advice should serve the client, not the advisor. As a fee-only, fiduciary firm, we are legally and ethically obligated to put your interests first, always. We earn no commissions. We take no kickbacks. You pay us directly, and in return you get advice that is completely, unambiguously on your side.

We have been helping Anne Arundel County families and business owners plan their financial futures for more than two decades. Our clients are our neighbors, and we take that relationship seriously.

"A financial plan is not about money. It is about the life you want to build."

What We Do

Comprehensive Wealth Management

Hover over any service to learn more about how we approach it.

Financial Planning

A comprehensive roadmap for your financial life.

We build a holistic plan that connects your income, savings, investments, insurance, and goals into one coherent picture. Updated annually and whenever your life changes.

Retirement Planning

Retire on your terms, not your account balance's.

We build projections that account for longevity, inflation, Social Security optimization, and sustainable withdrawal rates so you can retire with confidence.

Investment Management

Evidence-based, low-cost, globally diversified portfolios.

We manage your investments using academic research, not gut feeling. Low expense ratios, broad diversification, and disciplined rebalancing. No commissions, ever.

Tax Strategy

Minimize your lifetime tax burden, not just this year's.

We coordinate with your CPA on tax-loss harvesting, Roth conversions, asset location, and multi-year strategies designed to keep more of what you earn.

Estate Planning

Protect your family and your legacy.

We work alongside your estate attorney to ensure your beneficiaries, titling, trusts, and charitable intentions align with your wishes. Your legacy deserves a plan.

Business Owner Planning

Your business is your largest asset. Plan accordingly.

From retirement plan selection and cash flow management to exit planning and business valuation, we help business owners build wealth on both sides of the balance sheet.
