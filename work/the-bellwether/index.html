Our Story

A bar worth staying in.

The Bellwether started as a bet between two bartenders who believed Nashville deserved a place that took the craft seriously without taking itself too seriously. We opened in March 2021 in a converted 1940s storefront on 12th Ave South.

Every cocktail is built from scratch. We make our own syrups, bitters, and infusions, cut our own ice, and source spirits from small American distilleries whenever we can. The result is a menu that changes every season and a bar you actually want to sit at.

Every Week

What is on tonight.

We keep the calendar full. Whether you want a quiet booth or a reason to stay until close, there is always something worth showing up for.

  • Mon
    Quiet Service No music, just good drinks and conversation. Great for dates, catch-ups, or a solo night out.
  • Wed
    Jazz Sessions The Cedar Quartet plays from 8pm. Reservations strongly recommended.
  • Thu
    Guest Bartender Night A visiting bartender takes over the bar with their own menu. Always different, always worth coming for.
  • Fri & Sat
    Live Music from 9pm Rotating local artists. Full schedule posted each Monday on our Instagram.
  • Sun
    Sunday Brunch Service Brunch cocktails and light bites from noon. We close at 8pm.
