Our Philosophy

THIS IS NOT A FITNESS CENTER

Iron District was built in a converted warehouse on Nevins Street because we needed a place where the bar actually matters. We are a raw training facility for powerlifters, strongmen, Olympic lifters, boxers, and anyone willing to put in serious work under load.

No spin classes. No monthly challenges. No motivational posters. We have competition-grade equipment, world-class coaching, and a floor full of people who show up every day and do the work. If that is what you are looking for, you belong here.

What We Train

THE DISCIPLINES

01

Powerlifting

Full meet-prep programming for squat, bench, and deadlift. IPF-approved equipment. In-house competition lifting every quarter.

USAPL / IPF
02

Olympic Lifting

Snatch and clean-and-jerk coaching from USAW Level 3 certified staff. Jerk blocks, competition platforms, calibrated plates.

USAW CERTIFIED
03

Strongman

Atlas stones, yoke carry, log press, farmer carries, and tire flip. Full implements. Backyard contest prep available year-round.

WSM STANDARD
04

Boxing

Fundamentals through advanced striking. Four heavy bags, double-end bags, speed bags, and a full-size ring. NYBC certified instruction.

ALL LEVELS
05

Barbell Conditioning

High-intensity barbell complexes built for raw strength endurance. Not cardio with weights. Conditioning with a barbell.

METABOLIC STRENGTH
06

Functional Strength

Foundational athletic performance programming. Bilateral and unilateral loading, loaded carries, and movement quality under heavy load.
