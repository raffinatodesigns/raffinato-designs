The Philosophy

Built in a
Warehouse.
Built for War.

No chrome. No carpet. No playlist.

Iron District opened in 2013 in a converted industrial space off Wyckoff Avenue in Bushwick. We built it because Brooklyn had no serious training facility. Every box-gym in the borough catered to beginners with machines and group fitness classes. We went the other direction.

Our floor is 3,200 square feet of rubber, steel, chalk, and purpose. We train powerlifters, strongman competitors, Olympic lifters, boxers, and athletes who want to get strong without the nonsense. The bar is 400 lbs when you walk in. That is the standard.

What We Train

Programs

Hover each program. All levels welcome. Zero tolerance for bad form.

01 // Strength

Powerlifting

Competition prep and raw strength. Squat, bench, deadlift. Programs run 12-week cycles with RPE-based periodization.

02 // Power

Strongman

Atlas stones, farmer walks, log press, yoke carry, tire flips. Full implement setup on our outdoor pad.

03 // Technical

Olympic Lifting

Snatch and clean and jerk with dedicated platforms, bumper plates, and a coach who has stood on a podium.

04 // Combat

Boxing

Technical boxing and pad work. Footwork, combinations, defense. No sparring without coach clearance.

05 // Engine

Conditioning

GPP, metabolic work, cardio base development. Sleds, kettlebells, assault bike, and VO2 protocols.

06 // Access

Open Gym

24/7 badge access to the full floor. All equipment, chalk buckets, and a community that keeps to itself and lifts heavy.
