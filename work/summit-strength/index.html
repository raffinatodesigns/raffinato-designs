Our Story

Built For People Who Show Up

Summit Strength Co. opened its doors in Knoxville in 2019 with one goal: build a gym that takes your results as seriously as you do. No gimmicks, no overcrowded floors, no ignored check-ins.

Just professional coaching, premium equipment, and a community that pushes you to show up and get better every single day. Whether you're chasing your first pull-up or your next powerlifting meet, Summit is built for you.
