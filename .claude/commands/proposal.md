Draft a project proposal for a client. Ask me for any missing details before writing:

- Client name and business
- Project type (website, brand identity, landing page, etc.)
- Key goals or pain points they mentioned
- Budget range (if known)
- Timeline

Structure the proposal as:

1. **Overview** — one paragraph showing you understand their business and goals
2. **What We'll Deliver** — bulleted scope of work
3. **Our Process** — brief 3-4 step breakdown (Discovery → Design → Build → Launch)
4. **Investment** — pricing table with packages or line items
5. **Timeline** — estimated milestones
6. **Next Steps** — clear call to action

Tone: confident, warm, professional. Raffinato Designs positions as a premium boutique studio — avoid corporate filler language. Keep it under 2 pages.
