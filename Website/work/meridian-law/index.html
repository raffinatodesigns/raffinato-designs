About the Firm

Built on integrity.
Measured by results.

Founded in 1987 by Robert Hargrove and Eleanor Walsh, Meridian Law Group has grown from a two-attorney boutique into one of Chicago's most respected mid-size firms. We represent closely held businesses, real estate developers, families, and individuals navigating the complexities of the legal system.

We believe that exceptional legal counsel begins with listening. Every client who walks through our doors receives direct access to a senior attorney, clear communication throughout every phase of representation, and a commitment to outcomes that reflect their actual interests, not billable hours.

Integrity First

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear.

Direct Access

You work with a named partner, not a rotating associate team.

Clear Counsel

Plain language, no surprises, and a defined scope from day one.

Chicago Rooted

Deep relationships with local courts, agencies, and institutions.
